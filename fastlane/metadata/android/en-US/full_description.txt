A fast, clean PDF reader for Android that respects your privacy. No ads, no tracking, no account, and no internet access at all: the app does not even request the internet permission. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

PDF Viewer is free and open source. Anyone can read the code and check exactly what it does.

READ COMFORTABLY
• Continuous vertical scrolling or page-by-page horizontal paging
• Pinch and double-tap zoom with sharp, high-resolution rendering
• Page indicator and scrubber, go to page, thumbnails grid
• Table of contents (document outline) navigation
• Internal links jump to the right page; web links always ask before opening
• Night and sepia reading modes, light and dark themes, Material You dynamic colors
• Fullscreen reading, keep screen on, turn pages with the volume keys
• Picks up where you left off in every document
• Opens password-protected PDFs

FIND AND SELECT
• Full-text search with highlighted results
• Long-press to select text, then copy or share it

ANNOTATE
• Highlight, underline and strike through text
• Freehand ink drawing and sticky notes
• Eraser and undo
• Your original file is never modified: annotations are kept in the app until you export an annotated copy with standard PDF annotations that other readers can display

ORGANIZE
• Recent documents and favorites
• Add folders to your library to find all their PDFs in one place
• Per-document bookmarks

PDF TOOLS
• Merge several PDFs into one
• Split a PDF or extract pages
• Rotate, reorder and delete pages
• Turn images into a PDF
• Remove a password from a PDF you know the password for
• Compress PDFs
Tools always write a new file and never overwrite the original.

SHARE AND PRINT
• Open PDFs from your file manager, email or any app with "Open with" or "Share"
• Share, print, save a copy, view document properties

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: the app cannot send anything anywhere
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services, no third-party trackers
• No broad storage permission: the app only opens the files and folders you pick
• Your recent files, bookmarks, annotations and settings stay on your device

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
